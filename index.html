More Than Just a Sport

Leicester Judo Academy is a family friendly Judo Club and Fitness Centre promoting fun, fitness and discipline through quality Judo Instruction and Coaching. Leicester Judo Academy offers recreational and competitive Judo, tournaments, instructional courses, and training camps. When you come to visit, you’ll see why people in the Leicester area love to train here.

Also Offering 121 Personal Training and Small Group Fitness Training, Online Coaching and Nutritional Guidance from a fully qualified Personal Trainer with over 15 years experience in the industry
